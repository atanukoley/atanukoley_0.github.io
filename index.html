About Me

I am an astrophysicist currently working as a FONDECYT Postdoctoral Researcher at the Universidad de Concepción, Chile. I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, India, in 2022.

My research focuses on understanding the physical processes governing the interstellar medium (ISM) in the Milky Way, particularly the interplay between turbulence, magnetic fields, and star formation.

Research Interests

  • Interstellar Medium (ISM)
  • Magnetic Fields in Molecular Clouds
  • Magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) Turbulence
  • Massive Star Formation and Protoclusters
  • Atomic and Molecular Gas
  • Thermal Phase Distribution of the Neutral ISM
  • Velocity Gradient Techniques
