About Me

I am an astrophysicist currently working as a FONDECYT Postdoctoral Researcher at the Universidad de Concepción, Chile. I completed my PhD at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore, India, in 2022.

My research focuses on understanding the physical processes governing the interstellar medium (ISM) in the Milky Way, particularly how turbulence, magnetic fields, and gravity interact in shaping the structure, dynamics, and evolution of the multiphase ISM. I work at the interface between observational and theoretical astrophysics.

Research Interests

Multiphase Interstellar Medium: Thermal phase distribution and dynamics of neutral atomic gas in the Milky Way.
Turbulence and Magnetic Fields: Understanding the role of MHD turbulence and magnetic fields in the interstellar medium.
Star Formation: Physical processes governing massive star formation and protocluster evolution.
Observational and Theoretical Astrophysics: Combining radio and submillimetre observations with theoretical and numerical approaches.
