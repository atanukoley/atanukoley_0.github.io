Research

My research aims to understand the physical processes governing the interstellar medium (ISM) in the Milky Way, with particular emphasis on the interplay between turbulence, magnetic fields, and gravity in shaping the multiphase ISM.

Multiphase Interstellar Medium

I investigate the thermal phase distribution of the neutral interstellar medium, focusing on the cold, lukewarm, and warm neutral phases and their physical properties.

Turbulence and Magnetic Fields

I study the role of magnetohydrodynamic turbulence and magnetic fields in regulating the structure, dynamics, and evolution of the interstellar medium.

Massive Star Formation

I investigate the physical processes involved in massive star-forming regions and protoclusters, using radio and submillimetre observations.

Observational and Theoretical Astrophysics

My work combines observational studies with theoretical and numerical approaches to understand the complex physical processes operating in the interstellar medium.
